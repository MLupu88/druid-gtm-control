Improve the Signal Pulse visual.

Do not rebuild the app.
Do not redesign the whole app.
Do not change backend routes.
Do not expose secrets.
Do not call vendor APIs.
Do not change the shared contract.

Problem:
The current Signal Pulse line looks dull and decorative. It looks like a simple sine wave.
It should feel like a meaningful marketplace-interest pulse.

Goal:
Make Signal Pulse more visually intentional while staying professional and not sci-fi.

Changes:

1. Replace the smooth sine wave look

Use a pulse waveform that feels like signal activity:
- mostly calm baseline
- occasional sharp interest spikes
- quick recovery
- slight variation based on pulse score

It should not look medical.
It should not look like a generic audio waveform.
It should feel like “marketplace interest is moving”.

2. Make intensity dynamic

The waveform should visually change based on status:

Quiet:
- mostly flat
- low amplitude
- slow movement
- muted green

Building:
- moderate movement
- a few small spikes
- green/amber accent

Active:
- stronger movement
- visible spikes
- DRUID green

Hot:
- strongest movement
- brighter green glow
- more frequent spikes
- still tasteful, not aggressive

3. Improve styling

Use:
- thicker stroke than current
- subtle glow behind the line
- very light gradient fill under the pulse, if it looks good
- rounded line caps
- DRUID green as the main color

In light mode:
- line must be clearly visible
- do not make it neon on white
- use #00c158 or similar DRUID green

In dark mode:
- use #00e676 with subtle glow

4. Add a small intensity meter

Below or beside the line, add:

Interest strength: XX / 100

Use a slim progress bar:
- green fill
- muted background
- rounded corners

This makes the pulse feel measurable, not decorative.

5. Keep existing metrics

Keep:
- MQLs / Ready for Sales
- Worth a Look
- Signals captured

But make them visually cleaner, for example as small metric pills.

6. Keep copy business-like

Keep status copy:
Quiet: “Marketplace interest is quiet right now.”
Building: “Marketplace interest is building.”
Active: “Marketplace interest is active.”
Hot: “Marketplace interest is hot — review sales-ready accounts first.”

Do not call it heartbeat in the UI.

7. Accessibility

Respect prefers-reduced-motion:
- if reduced motion is enabled, show a static pulse line
- keep the same interest score and status text

8. Do not break logic

Do not change the scoring logic unless needed for visualization.
MQL / Ready for Sales must still exclude Worth a Look.
Blocked rows must not increase pulse.

9. Acceptance checks

After changes:
- typecheck frontend
- restart
- confirm Signal Pulse looks stronger in Sample data mode
- confirm it still works in Live data mode
- confirm it looks good in Light mode
- confirm it looks good in Dark mode
- confirm Interest strength score is visible
- report files changed
