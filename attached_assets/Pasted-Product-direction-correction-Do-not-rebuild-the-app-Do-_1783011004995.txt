Product direction correction.

Do not rebuild the app.
Do not redesign the app.
Do not change backend routes.
Do not call vendor APIs.
Do not expose secrets.
Do not change the shared contract unless absolutely required for UI labels.
Do not remove existing functionality.

The app purpose has become too generic. Reframe the product around the actual use case:

App name:
GTM Marketplace Signals

Purpose:
This app helps DRUID review marketplace and campaign buying signals, understand which accounts need attention, and decide whether to act, nurture, route, suppress, or export.

This is not a generic “Mission Control”.
This is not just a technical queue.
This is a marketplace/GTM signal review product.

Make these product copy and navigation changes:

1. Rename app header

Replace:
“Mission Control”

With:
“GTM Marketplace Signals”

In the sidebar header, use:
DRUID logo + “GTM Marketplace Signals”

Keep it visually clean. If the full title is too long, use:
“GTM Signals”
as the compact/collapsed label, but the full app title should be visible in normal sidebar mode.

2. Rename navigation

Current navigation:
- Dashboard
- Review Queue
- Reports
- Try a Sample Lead
- Settings

Change to:
- Overview
- Needs Your Attention
- Reports
- Settings

Remove “Try a Sample Lead” from the main sidebar navigation.

Do not delete the Try a Sample Lead page/functionality.
Move access to it into Settings under a small section called:
“Sample workflow”

Settings copy:

Button/link:
“Open sample workflow”

Overview

Keep internal code names if needed, but do not show them in the UI.

6. Add an MQL filter

On Needs Your Attention, add a clear filter for MQLs / Ready for Sales.

There is already “Ready for Sales”, but add an explicit compact filter chip:
“MQL / Ready for Sales”

It should match rows where:
- recommended_output is “MQL”
or
- OUTPUT_TYPE_LABELS maps the row to “Ready for Sales”
or
- score_tier / recommended_action indicates MQL / outbound-ready if using classic queue rows

Do not remove the existing “Ready for Sales” label, but make the filter understandable for sales/marketing users.
