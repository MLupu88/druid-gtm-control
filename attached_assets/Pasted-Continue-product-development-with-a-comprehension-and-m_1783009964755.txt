3. Dashboard: explain what the operator is looking at

Add a short explanatory panel near the top of Dashboard, below the title or state banner.

Title:
“What this page shows”

Copy:
“This dashboard shows which accounts need a human decision today. In Review Mode, approvals are recorded, but nothing is sent until sending is turned on.”

If Sample data mode is active, add:
“You are viewing sample accounts to understand the workflow. Switch to Live data to see the real queue.”

If Live data mode is active and queue is empty, add:
“Your live review queue is empty. That means there are no accounts waiting for a decision right now.”

Keep it concise and product-like.

5. Review Queue: explain what the operator is looking at

Add a short explanatory panel near the top of Review Queue.

Title:
“What this list shows”

Copy:
“Each row is an account or signal the GTM engine thinks may need action. The colored label on the left is the recommendation. The score is secondary — use the recommendation and the reason to decide what to do.”
